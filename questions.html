Open questions

What the map cannot settle from public text, and who can.

Not legal advice. Not official. Not for navigation.

Each question below is a point where the map cannot settle what the law means or where it applies, from public text alone. Most need an answer from the agency or city that enforces the rule. Until one arrives, the map draws the reading that closes more water and says so in the popup. Posted signs on site control over anything here. An answer sent in is checked against a primary source or the office that gave it before the map changes.

26 questions: 18 need a person, 8 are partly answered. Last updated 2026-11-12.

Each card says why the question matters on the map, the question to put, and who can answer it. A phone number appears only where it was read from that office's own published page.
